`POST /v1/pools/trades` · **1 credit**

The tape of up to 100+ markets in a single call, since a timestamp - grouped per market, per-market row cap. One request replaces a full polling fan-out.

<ParamField body="markets" type="array" required>
  List of `{chain, address}` pools (up to 200 per call). Example: `[{"chain":"evm:8453","address":"0x..."}]`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="from" type="integer" required>
  Only trades at/after this time (epoch ms) - at most 24h back (polling endpoint; page the per-market tape for history). Example: `1755000000000`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="perMarketLimit" type="integer">
  Rows per market (default 100, max 500). Example: `200`
</ParamField>
