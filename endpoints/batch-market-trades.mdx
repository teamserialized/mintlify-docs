`POST /v1/pools/trades` · **1 per market credit**

The tape of up to 200 markets in a single call, since a timestamp - the response groups trades per market and caps rows per market. One request replaces a whole polling fan-out. Rows are the exact [`/v1/pool/trades`](/endpoints/market-trades) shape. This is a polling endpoint (`from` is bounded to 24h back); page the per-market tape for deep history.

<ParamField body="markets" type="array" required>
  List of `{chain, address}` pools (up to 200 per call, cross-chain). Billing is per market. Example: `[{"chain":"evm:8453","address":"0x0ca6485b7e9cf814a3fd09d81672b07323535b64"}]`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="from" type="integer" required>
  Only trades at/after this time (epoch ms, inclusive) - at most 24h back. Example: `1786718000000`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="perMarketLimit" type="integer">
  Rows per market (default 100, max 500). Example: `200`
</ParamField>

<Note>Results come back in input order, one entry per requested market (`{chain, address, trades}`); a market with no trades in the window returns an empty `trades`. `meta.usdBasis` is `spot` here - over a ≤24h window the trade-time and spot rates coincide. Billed per market included in the call.</Note>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl -X POST 'https://api.serialized.xyz/v1/pools/trades' \
  -H 'Authorization: YOUR_API_KEY' -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"markets": [{"chain": "evm:8453", "address": "0x0ca6485b7e9cf814a3fd09d81672b07323535b64"}], "from": 1786718000000, "perMarketLimit": 200}'
```
</RequestExample>
