`GET /v1/audit/contract` · **750 credits**

Full Serialized Audit verdict for a contract: safety verdict, vulnerabilities, ownership/renounce state, proxy detection, open-source status. Synchronous: the reply always carries the verdict. An already-audited contract answers in milliseconds; a first-time audit holds until it completes.

<Note>The verdict sits under `data.audit`: `isSafe`, `isTokenSafe`, `isHookSafe`, `vulnerabilities[]` (each with `type`, `impact`, `mitigated`, `gateReason`), `sourceType`, `isProxy`, `implementationAddress`, `hookAddress`, `hookAudit`, `name`, `symbol`, `address`, `chain`, `createdAt`. Billed once per served verdict. A contract that has no liquidity pool yet answers `404`; retry once it trades.</Note>
