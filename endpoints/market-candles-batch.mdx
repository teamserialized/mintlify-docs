`POST /v1/pool/ohlcv` · **5 per item credits**

Batch twin of [Market Candles](/endpoints/market-candles): the candles of up to 10 markets in one call, chains mixable. `interval`, `limit`, `quote` and `fill` are shared by the lot; `endTime` and `from` are per market. Each slot is the body of the GET (`data` plus `count`, `oldestTime`, `newestTime`, `hasMore`, `nextEndTime`, `pools`), or `{ "error": { "code", "message" } }` for that market alone (`NOT_FOUND` for an unknown pool, `INVALID_PARAM` for a malformed address or `quote=usd` outside Solana). A slot is byte-identical to the GET of the same market with the same options.

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl -X POST 'https://api.serialized.xyz/v1/pool/ohlcv' \
  -H 'Authorization: YOUR_API_KEY' -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"items": [{"chain": "evm:8453", "address": "0x0ca6485b7e9cf814a3fd09d81672b07323535b64"}, {"chain": "solana", "address": "5zpyutJu9ee6jFymDGoK7F6S5Kczqtc9FomP3ueKuyA9"}], "interval": "5m", "limit": 1}'
```
</RequestExample>
