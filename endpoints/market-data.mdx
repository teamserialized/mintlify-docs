`GET /v1/pool/data` · **1 credit**

Live snapshot of one market, safe to poll at high frequency: price (native + USD), liquidity, market cap, circulating + total supply, and per-window stats - volume, buy/sell counts and volumes, unique traders, fees and price change. Windowed stats come from the same engine as [Token Stats](/endpoints/token-stats-windowed), scoped to this pool. The full security fields (authorities, taxes, concentration) live on [Token Security](/endpoints/token-security).

<ParamField query="chain" type="string" required>
  Public chain id - `evm:<id>` or `solana`. Example: `evm:8453`
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="address" type="string" required>
  Pool / pair address (the stable market id on its chain - Uniswap V4 poolIds supported). Example: `0x0ca6485b7e9cf814a3fd09d81672b07323535b64`
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="windows" type="string">
  Comma-separated stat windows: `5m`, `1h`, `6h`, `24h`. Default `5m,1h,24h`.
</ParamField>

<Note>Each window carries `volumeNative`/`volumeUsd`, `buys`/`sells`, `buyVolumeNative`/`sellVolumeNative`, `traders` (unique), `feesNative` and `priceChangePct` (close-referenced). Solana markets additionally carry `txns`, organic-volume twins and `poolFeesNative`.</Note>

<Note>`priceChangePct` is close-referenced: the change from the last close before the window opened.</Note>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl 'https://api.serialized.xyz/v1/pool/data?chain=evm:8453&address=0x0ca6485b7e9cf814a3fd09d81672b07323535b64&windows=5m,1h,24h' \
  -H 'Authorization: YOUR_API_KEY'
```
</RequestExample>
