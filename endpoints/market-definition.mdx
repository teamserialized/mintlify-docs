`GET /v1/pool` · **1 credit**

Static definition of one market (pool): venue/type, the launchpad or factory it came from, fee tier, base & quote token metadata, price, liquidity, launchpad + bonding state, and created-at. Slow-changing, so cache it freely. A market id is simply chain + pool address: stable, unique on its venue, never reused.

<Note>`factory` is the launchpad/factory of origin from our registry (e.g. `CLANKER`, `ZORA`, `PUMPFUN`), or `null` for a plain AMM pool. `feeBps` is the fee tier for concentrated-liquidity venues (Uniswap V3/V4, PancakeSwap V3, Aerodrome Slipstream); `null` when the fee is dynamic or the venue's fee units aren't certain, never a guess.</Note>

<Note>`bondingProgress` (0-100) is only present while `isLaunchpad` is true; `migrated` flips true once the token graduates to its AMM pool.</Note>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl 'https://api.serialized.xyz/v1/pool?chain=evm:8453&address=0x0ca6485b7e9cf814a3fd09d81672b07323535b64' \
  -H 'Authorization: YOUR_API_KEY'
```
</RequestExample>
