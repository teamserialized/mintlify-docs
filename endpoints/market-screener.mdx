`GET /v1/screener` · **2 credits**

Every tradable market, paginated: filters by chain and token, sort by volume, market cap or creation time, cursor pagination. The discovery primitive - poll it to detect new markets.

<ParamField query="chain" type="string">
  Restrict to one chain. Example: `evm:8453`
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="sortBy" type="string">
  `volume` (default), `marketCap` or `createdAt`. Example: `createdAt`
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="cursor" type="string">
  Opaque cursor from `meta.nextCursor`. Example: `eyJzIjo...`
</ParamField>
