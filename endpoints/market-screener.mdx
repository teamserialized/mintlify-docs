`GET /v1/screener` · **1 credit**

Every tradable market, paginated: filters by chain and token, sort by volume, market cap or creation time, cursor pagination. The discovery primitive - poll it to detect new markets.

<ParamField query="chains" type="string" required>
  CSV of public chain ids (1-8). sortBy=createdAt serves ONE chain per call (indexed walk) - fan out per chain. Example: `evm:8453,solana`
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="sortBy" type="string">
  `volume` (default), `marketCap` or `createdAt`. volume/marketCap rank within the top-1000-by-24h-volume universe (`meta.universe`). Example: `createdAt`
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer">
  Rows per page (default 30, max 100). Example: `50`
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="cursor" type="string">
  Opaque cursor from `meta.nextCursor`. Example: `eyJvIjozMH0`
</ParamField>
