`GET /v1/screener` · **1 credit**

Every tradable market, paginated: filters by chain and token, sort by volume, market cap or creation time, cursor pagination. The discovery primitive - poll it to detect new markets.

<Note>Each row is a market (chain + pool): token and quote identity, `priceNative`/`priceUsd`, `marketCapUsd`, `liquidityUsd`, `volume24hUsd`, `priceChange24hPct`, `txns`, `buys`, `sells`, `createdAt`.</Note>

<Note>For `volume`/`marketCap`, rows are ranked within a warmed universe of the top markets by 24h USD volume, named in `meta.universe` (e.g. `top1000ByVolume24hUsd`); `sortBy=createdAt` is a live indexed walk of newest markets on one chain.</Note>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl 'https://api.serialized.xyz/v1/screener?chains=evm:8453,solana&sortBy=volume&limit=2' \
  -H 'Authorization: YOUR_API_KEY'
```
</RequestExample>
