`GET /v1/token` · **1 credit**

Full token snapshot: price (native + USD), market cap, FDV and supplies (total, circulating), liquidity (+ATH), best pool (canonical priority ranking), launchpad state (bonding progress, graduation), deployer, 24h stats, metadata & socials.

<Note>`totalSupply` is the on-chain supply of the contract on the requested chain. `marketCapUsd` and `fdvUsd` are the asset's circulating market cap and fully diluted value: for an asset issued on several chains (stablecoins, bridged majors) they cover the asset as a whole, across every chain, while `totalSupply` stays per contract. `circulatingSupply` is `null` when the token has no distinct circulating supply; `marketCapUsd` then equals `fdvUsd` (`totalSupply` × price). Wrapped natives (WETH, WBNB, WgUSDT...) and the chain's native coin (`0x000...000` on EVM, the SOL mint on Solana) are priced at `priceNative` `"1"` by construction, `priceUsd` being the same spot as [`/v1/prices/native`](/endpoints/native-prices). A wrapped native carries no capitalisation of its own: its `totalSupply`, `circulatingSupply`, `marketCapUsd` and `fdvUsd` are `null`; the coin's own figures live on the native coin entry. Its liquidity and `stats24h` are the wrapper contract's markets.</Note>

<Note>`pool` is the token's best market under the canonical priority ranking: the same pool as the first row of [`/v1/token/pools`](/endpoints/token-pools).</Note>

<Note>`priceNative`, `priceUsd`, `marketCapUsd`, `fdvUsd`, `liquidityNative`, `liquidityUsd`, `priceNativeAth` and `liquidityNativeAth` are `null` when the token has not traded yet; `stats24h` then reports measured zeros. A token whose price fell to zero after trading keeps `priceNative` `"0"`.</Note>

<Note>`createdAt` is the TRUE deploy time (launchpad pool) - `launchpad.graduatedAt` carries the migration.</Note>

<Note>`deployer` is the token's creator wallet: the launchpad creator when the token has a launchpad lineage, otherwise the wallet that created its canonical pool - never the migration transaction sender. `deployerSource` says which arm resolved it (`metadata` when the launchpad's metadata feed names the creator, `launchpad` for the creator of the original bonding curve, `pool` for the creator of the canonical pool) and is `null` whenever `deployer` is `null`.</Note>

<Note>`stats24h` is the token-wide 24h window, the same window as the `24h` block of [`/v1/token/stats`](/endpoints/token-stats-windowed). `buys`/`sells` and `buyVolumeNative`/`sellVolumeNative` split flow by direction; `txns` is the number of distinct transactions in the window. `priceChangePct` is the price change over the 24h window, in percent. `null` when the window carries no priced trades.</Note>

<Note>`metadata.dexPaid` is `true`, `false`, or `null` (status unknown) - never a fabricated `false`. When our index has no record for a token, the status is resolved from DEX Screener's listing records. `metadata.dexPaidAt` is the payment time in ms, or `null`.</Note>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL (EVM)
curl 'https://api.serialized.xyz/v1/token?chain=evm:8453&address=0x4ed4e862860bed51a9570b96d89af5e1b0efefed' \
  -H 'Authorization: YOUR_API_KEY'
```
```bash cURL (Solana)
curl 'https://api.serialized.xyz/v1/token?chain=solana&address=DezXAZ8z7PnrnRJjz3wXBoRgixCa6xjnB7YaB1pPB263' -H 'Authorization: YOUR_API_KEY'
```
