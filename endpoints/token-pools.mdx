`GET /v1/token/pools` · **1 credit**

The markets of a token, ranked by our canonical pool priority - rank 1 is the exact pool the token endpoint prices from. A token is listed on both sides of its markets: as the priced asset of a pool (`side: "base"`) and on the markets where it is the quote asset (`side: "quote"`), which is how wrapped natives and stablecoins such as WETH, WHYPE or USDC trade.

<Note>Each row describes the pool from its own viewpoint: `baseAddress` / `baseSymbol` is the pool's priced asset, `quoteAddress` / `quoteSymbol` its quote asset, `priceNative` the price of the base asset in the chain's native coin, and `side` tells you which of the two is the token you asked for. The token you asked for is always identified; the other side is `null` while its identity is still being resolved.</Note>

<Note>Each pool carries its own 24h stats - `volume24hNative`, `volume24hUsd`, `txns24h` - so you can pick the market to trade or price against. `null` when a pool has no 24h activity to report.</Note>

<Note>`priceNative` and `liquidityNative` are `null` for a pool that has not traded yet. A pool whose price fell to zero after trading keeps `priceNative` `"0"`.</Note>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl 'https://api.serialized.xyz/v1/token/pools?chain=evm:8453&address=0x4ed4e862860bed51a9570b96d89af5e1b0efefed' \
  -H 'Authorization: YOUR_API_KEY'
```
</RequestExample>
