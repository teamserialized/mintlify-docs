`GET /v1/token/security` · **10 credits**

Everything you need to screen a token before trading it, in one call and one shape across EVM and Solana:

- **Authorities** - `mintAuthority` / `mintRevoked`, `freezeAuthority` / `freezeRevoked`: an active mint or freeze authority is the classic honeypot flag.
- **Concentration** - `holdersCount`, `top10HoldersPct` (CEX-custody and pool/burn wallets excluded - the exclusion *is* the signal), `devHoldingsPct`.
- **Launch quality** - `snipersHoldingsPct`, `bundlersHoldingsPct`, `tokenBurnedPct`.
- **Liquidity safety** - `lpBurnedPct`, `lpLockedPct`, `lpSource`.
- **Taxes & listing** - `buyTaxBps`, `sellTaxBps`, `transferTaxBps`, `dexPaid`, `dexPaidAt` (the DEX Screener listing status, resolved from its listing records when our index has no record).

`null` always means unknown, never a fabricated zero or false.

<Note>Authority tri-state: `mintRevoked: true` means the authority is renounced (safe), `false` means it is still active, `null` means unknown. Same for freeze.</Note>

<Note>Available as a batch: `POST /v1/token/security` with `items[]` - up to 100 items per call, across chains.</Note>
