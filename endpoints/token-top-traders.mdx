`GET /v1/token/top-traders` · **2 credits**

Ranked traders of a token from our own PnL fold: realized PnL in native and USD, tokens and native bought/sold, open position, average entry, win rate over closed positions, first/last trade, and dev/sniper/pro-trader badges. Lifetime by default, or any rolling window (`1h` → `30d`). EVM and Solana, one shape.

<ParamField query="chain" type="string" required>
  Public chain id - `evm:<id>` or `solana`. Example: `evm:8453`
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="address" type="string" required>
  Token address. Example: `0x3c5cd672b204ba0fc48e93b98c0922920a87912d`
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="timeframe" type="string">
  `all` (default, lifetime) or a rolling window: `1h`, `4h`, `1d`, `7d`, `30d`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="sortBy" type="string">
  `pnl` (default), `bought`, `sold`, or `txns`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="order" type="string">
  `asc` or `desc`. Default: `desc`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" type="number">
  1-100. Default 20.
</ParamField>

<Note>`meta` echoes the resolved `timeframe`, `sortBy` and `order`, plus `usdBasis` - the basis of every USD figure in the response.</Note>

<Note>On a rolling window, `realizedPnlNative` is the wallet's net native cashflow over the window (sold − bought) and `winRatePct` is `null`; win rate is measured over fully closed lifetime positions (`timeframe=all`).</Note>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl 'https://api.serialized.xyz/v1/token/top-traders?chain=evm:8453&address=0x3c5cd672b204ba0fc48e93b98c0922920a87912d&limit=3' \
  -H 'Authorization: YOUR_API_KEY'
```
</RequestExample>
