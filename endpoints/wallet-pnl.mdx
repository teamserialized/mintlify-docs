`GET /v1/wallet/pnl` · **1 credit**

Realized PnL of a wallet: daily curve plus summary (total realized, win rate, trades, wins/losses). Omit `chain` to combine every EVM chain; `chain=solana` for Solana.

<Note>Solana responses carry `usdBasis`: `trade` (each day valued at that day's native/USD rate) or `spot` (valued at the current native price). EVM PnL is valued at the current native price.</Note>

<Note>`period` keeps the UTC days from now minus that many days through today, both inclusive: `7d` spans 8 calendar days, `1d` yesterday and today, `all` the whole history.</Note>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl 'https://api.serialized.xyz/v1/wallet/pnl?wallet=0x95d955179a7cd45aeef394ed39f6a8d8b1bd1e09&period=7d' \
  -H 'Authorization: YOUR_API_KEY'
```
</RequestExample>
