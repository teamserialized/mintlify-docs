`GET /v1/wallet/positions` · **2 credits**

Every position of a wallet with entry price and PnL: amount held, USD value, average entry, realized + unrealized + total PnL, trade counts and first/last trade timestamps. EVM and Solana.

<Note>Lists the tokens currently held that the wallet has traded. `entryPriceUsd` is the current bag's average entry; `entryPriceLifetimeUsd` is the average over the position's whole life. The realized counterpart of each fully-exited position is on [`/v1/wallet/closed-positions`](/endpoints/wallet-closed-positions) - together the two cover every position exactly once.</Note>

<Note>`meta.summary` carries wallet-level totals (value, realized, unrealized, total PnL, position count). Returns 502 if a holdings source is unavailable.</Note>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl 'https://api.serialized.xyz/v1/wallet/positions?chain=solana&wallet=GuhieZDx6nwJV14WjeUQH2ted6CGPKoGCCTaPSPNgG2E' \
  -H 'Authorization: YOUR_API_KEY'
```
</RequestExample>
