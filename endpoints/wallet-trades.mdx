`GET /v1/wallet/trades` · **1 credit**

Trade history of a wallet, newest first - multihop-deduplicated (one event per transaction, token and side; never doubled volumes). Paginate with the opaque `cursor` from `meta.nextCursor` on both EVM and Solana: exact keyset pagination that never skips or repeats a trade. Routed intermediate legs (the wSOL or stable a route passes through) are excluded by default and flagged `isRoutingHop`; on Solana pass `includeRoutingHops=true` to include them. Wash/MEV rows are kept and carry the `isWash` badge (Solana) so consumers choose.

<Note>**Pagination.** Each page is bounded by `limit` and by a scan window over the wallet's history: a page can hold fewer than `limit` rows while `meta.hasMore` is `true`. Keep passing `meta.nextCursor` until `hasMore` is `false`; cursors never skip or repeat a trade, and `hasMore: false` means the wallet's history is fully served.</Note>

<Note>Each trade carries `feesNative` (the transaction fee in the chain's native unit), `isRoutingHop` (a routed intermediate leg) and `isWash` (Solana wash/MEV flag; `null` on EVM). `volumeUsd` is at the current native price on EVM; on Solana it is valued at trade time and `meta.usdBasis` reports `trade` (or `spot` on fallback). `meta.nextCursor` is present while `meta.hasMore` is true.</Note>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL (EVM)
curl 'https://api.serialized.xyz/v1/wallet/trades?chain=evm:8453&wallet=0x95d955179a7cd45aeef394ed39f6a8d8b1bd1e09&limit=2' \
  -H 'Authorization: YOUR_API_KEY'
```
```bash cURL (Solana)
curl 'https://api.serialized.xyz/v1/wallet/trades?chain=solana&wallet=GuhieZDx6nwJV14WjeUQH2ted6CGPKoGCCTaPSPNgG2E&limit=2' \
  -H 'Authorization: YOUR_API_KEY'
```
</RequestExample>
