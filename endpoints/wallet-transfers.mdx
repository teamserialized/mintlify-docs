`GET /v1/wallet/transfers` · **1 credit**

Token in/out history of a wallet: deposits, withdrawals, plain transfers - timestamp (ms), token, amount, direction, counterparty, tx hash. The reconciliation primitive next to `/v1/wallet/trades`.

<ParamField query="chain" type="string" required>
  Public chain id - `evm:<id>` or `solana`. Example: `evm:8453`
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="wallet" type="string" required>
  Wallet address. Example: `0x8888888888888888888888888888888888888888`
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer">
  Transactions scanned (default 50, max 100). Example: `50`
</ParamField>

<Note>`direction` is `in`, `out` or `null`; `counterparty` is the other side of the transfer. Amounts are strings for precision; `amountUsd` is valued at transfer time.</Note>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl 'https://api.serialized.xyz/v1/wallet/transfers?chain=evm:8453&wallet=0x95d955179a7cd45aeef394ed39f6a8d8b1bd1e09&limit=2' \
  -H 'Authorization: YOUR_API_KEY'
```
</RequestExample>
